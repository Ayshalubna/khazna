# Khazna — private document assistant

Live demo of [github.com/Ayshalubna/khazna](https://github.com/Ayshalubna/khazna).
Ask questions about confidential company documents in English or Arabic. Answers cite their sources, only use
documents your role may read, hide personal data, ignore instructions planted in documents, and are written by a
small open model running inside this Space, with outbound network blocked after start-up.
All documents, people and numbers are fictional.
