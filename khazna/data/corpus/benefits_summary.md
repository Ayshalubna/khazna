# Benefits Summary 2026

## Medical insurance

All employees and their spouse and up to three children are covered by the company medical insurance plan. Grades G1 to G4 are on the Silver network; grades G5 and above are on the Gold network, which includes treatment abroad.

## Annual air ticket

Employees receive one economy return air ticket to their home country each year, or a cash allowance of AED 2,500 if they prefer.

## Wellness

A wellness allowance of AED 250 per month is paid with salary and can be used for a gym, sports club or fitness app.

## Education allowance

Employees in grade G6 and above receive an education allowance of up to AED 20,000 per child per year, for a maximum of two children.

## End-of-service gratuity

End-of-service gratuity is paid in line with UAE labour regulations. Questions about your own gratuity estimate should be sent to the HR Service Desk.

## Pension

UAE national employees are registered with the relevant pension authority; the company pays the employer contribution in full.
