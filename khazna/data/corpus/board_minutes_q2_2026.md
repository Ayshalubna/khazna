# Board Meeting Minutes, Q2 2026

Restricted. Board members and the Executive Committee only. Meeting held on 18 June 2026 in Dubai.

## Results

Revenue for the first half of 2026 was AED 212 million, 9% above the same period last year. Operating margin was 11.4%.

## New warehouse

The Board approved a new 24,000 square metre warehouse in KEZAD, Abu Dhabi, with a budget of AED 18.5 million. Operations are planned to start in the first quarter of 2027.

## Project Falcon

The Board authorised management to continue confidential talks to acquire Bayan Cold Chain, under the code name Project Falcon. A non-binding offer of up to AED 64 million may be made. No announcement will be made before the Board's approval of a final offer.

## Leadership

The Board confirmed the appointment of Rania Haddad as Chief Financial Officer from 1 August 2026.

## Next meeting

The next Board meeting will be held on 17 September 2026.
