# Master Services Agreement: Gulf Retail Trading LLC

Summary of the key terms of the agreement between Sadeem Freight LLC and Gulf Retail Trading LLC (both fictional), signed on 12 December 2025.

## Term

The agreement runs for three years from 1 January 2026 to 31 December 2028, and renews automatically for one year at a time unless either party gives notice.

## Services

Sadeem Freight provides warehousing in Jebel Ali and last-mile delivery to Gulf Retail's 38 stores across the UAE.

## Service level

At least 97% of store deliveries must arrive within the agreed time window each month. For each full percentage point below 97%, Gulf Retail receives a service credit of 2% of that month's fees, capped at 10% of the monthly fees.

## Fees and payment

The monthly fixed fee is AED 1.2 million plus variable charges per pallet. Gulf Retail pays invoices within 60 days of receipt.

## Termination

Either party may terminate the agreement for convenience with 90 days' written notice after the first year. Either party may terminate immediately for a material breach that is not fixed within 30 days of written notice.

## Liability

Each party's total liability in any contract year is capped at the total fees paid in the previous 12 months, except for fraud or wilful misconduct.

## Governing law

The agreement is governed by the laws of the Emirate of Dubai and the federal laws of the UAE. Disputes go to the courts of Dubai.
