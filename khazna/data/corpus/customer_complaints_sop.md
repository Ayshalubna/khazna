# Customer Complaints Procedure

## Acknowledgement

Every customer complaint is logged in the CRM and acknowledged within 4 working hours.

## Resolution targets

Standard complaints are resolved within 3 working days. Complaints about damaged goods are resolved within 7 working days after the claim form and photos are received.

## Compensation

Customer service agents may approve goodwill credits up to AED 500. Larger amounts need the approval of the Customer Service Manager.

## Escalation

Complaints from key accounts, including Gulf Retail Trading, are escalated to the account manager on the same day.
