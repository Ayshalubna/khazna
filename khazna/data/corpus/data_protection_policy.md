# Personal Data Protection Policy

Sadeem Freight processes personal data about employees, customers and the recipients of shipments. This policy sets out how that data is protected.

## Data Protection Officer

The Data Protection Officer (DPO) is Mariam Al Suwaidi, who can be reached at dpo@sadeem-freight.example.

## Where data is stored

Customer and employee personal data is stored only in data centres located in the UAE. Transferring personal data outside the UAE requires written approval from the DPO.

## How long data is kept

Employee records are kept for five years after the employee leaves. Financial and accounting records are kept for seven years. Shipment recipient details are deleted 12 months after delivery.

## Breaches

Any suspected personal data breach must be reported to the DPO within 24 hours of discovery. The DPO decides whether customers, employees or regulators need to be informed.

## Access requests

Employees may request a copy of the personal data the company holds about them. Requests are answered within 30 days.

## AI tools

Personal data may only be processed by AI systems that run inside the company's own environment and have been approved by the DPO.
