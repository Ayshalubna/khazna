# Employee Handbook 2026

Sadeem Freight LLC (fictional company used for this demo) employs 420 people across Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Sharjah. This handbook applies to all full-time employees from 1 January 2026.

## Working hours

The standard working week is Monday to Friday, 8:00 am to 5:00 pm, with a one-hour lunch break. Warehouse shift staff follow the roster published by their site manager every Thursday.

During the holy month of Ramadan, office working hours are reduced to 9:00 am to 3:00 pm for all employees, whether or not they are fasting.

## Probation and notice

New employees serve a probation period of six months. During probation either side may end employment with 14 days' written notice. After probation the notice period is 30 days for grades G1 to G4 and 60 days for grades G5 and above.

## Annual leave

Employees earn 2.5 working days of annual leave per month in their first year. From the second year onwards the entitlement is 30 calendar days per year. Up to 10 unused days may be carried over to the next year; carried-over days must be used by 31 March or they are lost.

Leave requests must be submitted in the HR portal at least 14 days in advance for absences of five days or more.

## Sick leave

Sick leave requires a medical certificate from a licensed UAE clinic for any absence longer than one day. The first 15 days of sick leave in a year are paid in full, the next 30 days at half pay, and any further days are unpaid.

## Remote work

Office-based employees may work from home up to two days per week with their manager's approval. Remote work is not available during the first three months of employment, and is not available to warehouse and driver roles.

## Public holidays

Sadeem Freight observes the official UAE public holidays announced by the government each year. Employees who are required to work on a public holiday receive a replacement day off, to be taken within 60 days.

## Dress code

Business casual in offices. Safety boots and the high-visibility vest are mandatory in all warehouse areas, including for visitors.
