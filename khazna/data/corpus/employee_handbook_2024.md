# Employee Handbook 2024

This version was replaced by the Employee Handbook 2026 on 1 January 2026. It is kept for reference only.

## Working hours

The standard working week was Monday to Friday, 8:30 am to 5:30 pm. During Ramadan, office hours were 9:00 am to 2:00 pm.

## Annual leave

Employees received 22 working days of annual leave per year. Up to 5 unused days could be carried over.

## Remote work

Remote work was allowed one day per week for office staff after probation.

## Probation

The probation period was three months.
