# Fleet and Driver Policy

## Company vehicles

Company trucks and vans may only be driven by employees holding a valid UAE driving licence for that vehicle class. Vehicles must not be used for personal trips.

## Fuel

Company vehicles are refuelled with the company fuel card only. Fuel card receipts must be uploaded in the fleet app on the same day.

## Traffic fines

Traffic fines incurred while driving a company vehicle are the responsibility of the driver and are deducted from salary after the driver is informed in writing. Salik tolls on company routes are paid by the company.

## Accidents

After any accident, the driver must call the police, take photos and inform the fleet supervisor within one hour.

## Rest periods

Drivers must take a break of at least 30 minutes after every four and a half hours of driving.
