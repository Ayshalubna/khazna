# Grievance and Whistleblowing Policy

## Raising a grievance

An employee with a work-related complaint should first raise it with their line manager. If it is not resolved within 10 working days, they may submit a written grievance to HR.

## Whistleblowing

Concerns about fraud, bribery or safety may be reported confidentially to the Ethics Line at ethics@sadeem-freight.example. Reports can be made anonymously.

## Protection

No employee will be disadvantaged for raising a concern in good faith.

## Investigation

HR or Legal acknowledges a written grievance within 5 working days and aims to complete an investigation within 30 days.
