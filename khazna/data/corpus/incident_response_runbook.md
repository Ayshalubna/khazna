# Security Incident Response Runbook

## Severity levels

- P1 Critical: customer data exposed, ransomware, or the warehouse management system is down. Response within 15 minutes, 24/7.
- P2 High: a single business system is unavailable or a confirmed malware infection on one device. Response within one hour.
- P3 Medium: suspicious activity with no confirmed impact. Response within one business day.

## Escalation

For P1 incidents, the on-call engineer must call the Chief Information Security Officer, Omar Khalid, on +971 50 123 4567, and open a bridge call within 30 minutes. The DPO must be informed of any incident involving personal data.

## First steps for ransomware

1. Disconnect the affected devices from the network but do not switch them off.
2. Disable the affected user accounts.
3. Preserve logs from the firewall and endpoint protection console.
4. Do not contact the attackers or pay any ransom.

## After the incident

A written post-incident review is due within five business days of closing a P1 or P2 incident.
