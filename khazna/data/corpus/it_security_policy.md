# Information Security Policy

Every Sadeem Freight employee and contractor must follow this policy.

## Passwords and sign-in

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for email, the HR portal, the Rihla portal and remote access. Passwords are changed only when there is a sign of compromise; routine forced changes are not used.

## Data classification

Company information has four levels: Public, Internal, Confidential and Restricted. Confidential and Restricted information must never be uploaded to public AI tools, personal email or personal cloud storage.

## Devices

All company laptops are encrypted with BitLocker and must lock after five minutes of inactivity. USB storage drives are blocked on all company devices. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the IT Service Desk within two hours.

## Phishing

Report suspicious emails with the "Report phishing" button in Outlook, or forward them to security@sadeem-freight.example, within one hour. Never enter your password on a page you reached from an email link.

## Incidents

To report a security incident at any time, call the 24/7 security hotline on extension 7777.

## Breaches of this policy

Breaching this policy may lead to disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment.
