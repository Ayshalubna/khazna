# Procurement Policy

## Quotes and approvals

Purchases below AED 10,000 need one written quote and the approval of the budget holder. Purchases from AED 10,000 to AED 100,000 need three written quotes and the approval of the Finance Manager. Purchases above AED 100,000 must go through a formal tender reviewed by the Tender Committee.

## Tender Committee

The Tender Committee is chaired by the Chief Financial Officer and includes the Head of Legal and the Head of Operations. A tender needs at least three qualified bidders unless the committee approves a single-source exception in writing.

## Payment terms

Standard supplier payment terms are 45 days from the date the invoice is received. Shorter terms need the approval of the Finance Director.

## Conflicts of interest

Anyone involved in a purchase must declare any personal or family relationship with a supplier before quotes are requested. Gifts from suppliers above AED 200 in value must be declined.

## Supplier onboarding

New suppliers must provide a valid trade licence, a VAT registration certificate and bank details on company letterhead. Bank detail changes are always verified by phone with a known contact before any payment.
