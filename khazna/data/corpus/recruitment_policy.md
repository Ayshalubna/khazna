# Recruitment Policy

## Approved positions

A vacancy can only be advertised once it is approved in the annual headcount plan or by the Chief Executive Officer.

## Interviews

Every candidate for grade G4 and above is interviewed by at least two people, including one from HR.

## Referral bonus

Employees who refer a candidate who is hired and passes probation receive a referral bonus of AED 3,000.

## Emiratisation

Recruitment plans must include the company's Emiratisation targets for skilled roles.

## Background checks

Offers are conditional on reference checks and, for finance roles, a credit and background check.
