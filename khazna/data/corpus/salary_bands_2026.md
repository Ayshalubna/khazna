# Salary Bands and Pay Review 2026

Restricted. For Human Resources and the Executive Committee only.

## Monthly basic salary bands (AED)

- G1 Warehouse associate: 3,000 to 4,500
- G2 Driver and senior associate: 4,500 to 6,500
- G3 Coordinator: 6,500 to 9,500
- G4 Specialist: 9,500 to 14,000
- G5 Manager: 14,000 to 22,000
- G6 Senior manager: 22,000 to 32,000
- G7 Director: 32,000 to 45,000
- G8 Executive: 45,000 to 70,000

Housing allowance is 25% of basic salary and transport allowance is 10% of basic salary.

## 2026 pay review

The merit increase budget for 2026 is 4% of total basic payroll. Increases take effect on 1 April 2026. Bonus targets are 10% of annual basic salary for G5 and G6, and 20% for G7 and G8.
