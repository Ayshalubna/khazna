# Learning and Development Policy

## Training budget

Each employee may use up to AED 5,000 per year for approved courses and certifications related to their role.

## Approval

Training requests are approved by the line manager and then by HR. Requests above AED 3,000 also need the department head's approval.

## Repayment

If an employee leaves within 12 months of completing a course that cost more than AED 3,000, they repay the cost on a pro-rata basis.

## Mandatory training

All employees complete information security awareness training and the code of conduct course every year.
