# Travel and Expense Policy

This policy covers business travel and expense claims for all Sadeem Freight employees.

## Booking

All business travel is booked through the Rihla travel portal at least 10 days before departure. Bookings made outside the portal are not reimbursed unless approved in writing by the Finance Director.

## Flights

Employees fly economy class. Directors and above may fly business class on flights longer than six hours.

## Hotels

The nightly hotel limit is AED 900 in Dubai and Abu Dhabi, AED 750 elsewhere in the GCC, and USD 250 in all other countries, excluding taxes.

## Daily allowance

A daily allowance (per diem) covers meals and local transport: AED 450 per day for travel within the GCC and AED 600 per day for travel outside the GCC. The allowance is not paid on days when meals are provided by the host.

## Car mileage

Employees who use their own car for business travel are reimbursed AED 0.60 per kilometre, plus Salik tolls and parking with receipts.

## Claims

Expense claims must be submitted in the Rihla portal within 30 days of the end of the trip. An original or digital receipt is required for every item above AED 100. Claims are paid with the next monthly salary after approval by the line manager and Finance.

## Not reimbursed

Alcohol, traffic fines, personal entertainment, minibar charges and upgrades purchased by the employee are never reimbursed.
