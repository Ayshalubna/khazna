# Warehouse Health and Safety Procedures

## Personal protective equipment

Safety boots and high-visibility vests are required in every warehouse area. Hard hats are required in racking zones above three metres.

## Forklifts

Only certified operators may drive forklifts. Forklift certification must be renewed every two years.

## Summer working hours

Under the UAE midday break rule, no outdoor work is done between 12:30 pm and 3:00 pm from 15 June to 15 September.

## Reporting

All accidents and near misses must be reported to the site safety officer within 24 hours using the safety form.

## Fire

Fire drills are held every six months at every site. Fire exits must never be blocked by pallets.
